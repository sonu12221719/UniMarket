# UniMarket

![UniMarket Preview](./unimarket-ss.png)

**UniMarket** is a platform for college and university students to **buy and sell second-hand products** easily within their campus community. Whether it's books, electronics, furniture, or other essentials — UniMarket helps students connect with others to find what they need or sell what they no longer use.

### Key Features:
- Post your products with images, descriptions, and prices
- Browse items listed by other students
- Contact sellers directly to make deals
- Clean and user-friendly interface

Perfect for students who want to save money or make some extra cash!
